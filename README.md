# Ashwagg's Trial Skin

Ashwagg's Trial Skin is a unique Minecraft texture pack that transforms your player model into a distinctive character with a netherite chestplate, copper eye trim, and a trial key. This skin is designed to stand still and features a progression from netherite to iron to gold, with the eye trim remaining constant. It's perfect for players who want a unique and recognizable character in their Minecraft world.

## Install

1. Download the Ashwagg's Trial Skin texture pack from the GitHub repository.
2. Open Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
3. Go to the "Skins" section in the game menu.
4. Click on the "+" button to add a new skin.
5. Select the downloaded Ashwagg's Trial Skin texture pack.
6. Choose the skin and apply it to your character.

## Use

1. Once the skin is applied, your character will have the Ashwagg's Trial Skin model.
2. The skin features a netherite chestplate, copper eye trim, and a trial key.
3. The trial key will give you the netherite chestplate and copper eye trim.
4. When you drop the trimmed chestplate, it will progress from iron to gold.
